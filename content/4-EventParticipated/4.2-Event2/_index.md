# Report on "Workshop: Cloud & AI Trends - Preparing for Students"

### Event Objectives

- Update current Cloud & AI market trends
- Equip students with necessary skills and mindset for the AI wave
- Connect students with industry experts

### Speaker List

- **Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung** - Head of SA, AWS Vietnam
- **Mr. Banh Cam Vinh** - Renova Cloud
- **Ms. Nhu Tran** - Account Manager, AWS Vietnam
- **Mr. Khang Nguyen** - Cloud Kinetics, Swinburne alumni

### Key Content

#### 1. Job Market & Cloud Trends (Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung - Head of SA, AWS Vietnam)

##### Current Market Reality:

- **Intern positions already require Kubernetes + Container** – one intern failed a bank interview because they didn't understand K8S
- Another intern had good English, certifications, solid foundation but was still evaluated as **"non"** – businesses no longer have patience with Freshers
- **90% of jobs are not publicly posted**, but through referrals and internal networks. That's why joining the community is essential.

##### AWS in Vietnam:

- AWS has grown **20 times in 6 years**, while traditional hardware infrastructure has decreased 10 times
- **Cloud first** – every new application must be designed to run on cloud first, only moved on-premise when truly necessary (like CCTV)
- AWS commits to long-term investment with **3 pillars**:
  - **Local talent** – 99.9% of AWS Vietnam employees are Vietnamese
  - **Infrastructure** – investing in Local Zone in Hanoi (hundreds of millions USD), aiming for AWS Region (6+ billion USD, 15-20 years to break even)
  - **Skill & Future Talent** – building the future builder workforce for Vietnam

##### 6 major industries using AWS:

| Industry | Example Customers |
| --- | --- |
| Banking & Fintech | Banks, finance, insurance |
| Retail & E-commerce | Retail, e-commerce |
| Manufacturing | Production |
| Media & Entertainment | VTV, entertainment |
| Telco | Telecommunications |
| Healthcare | Medical |

##### Advice:

Besides choosing a role (Data Eng, DevOps, AI), students should **also choose an industry** to make their profile stand out.

##### Inverted pyramid model:

- **Before:** 3-5 Junior → 2-3 Middle → 1 Senior
- **Now:** 1-2 Junior → AI Agent replaces Middle → More Seniors

Junior demand decreases, but total industry opportunities don't decrease – the market is just forcing you to **mature faster**.

##### Success formula:

> **Result = Capability × Visibility × Consistency**

- **Capability:** Professional competence
- **Visibility:** Presence, network relationships
- **Consistency:** Persistence, continuous learning

> *"Certificates only help you pass the CV screening. AI will scan your CV, scan your side projects, scan your blog. How prepared is your profile?"*

##### LED light bulb example:

> LED bulbs save 90% of electricity, but global electricity consumption doesn't decrease – because people use more lights, illuminate more places

Same with AI: **software becomes cheaper → software demand increases → job opportunities increase in the long run**

#### 2. Data Engineering – University vs Business (Mr. Banh Cam Vinh - Renova Cloud)

##### 5 important foundational subjects:

1. Databases
2. Programming Languages
3. Data Structures & Algorithms
4. Distributed Databases
5. BI (Business Intelligence)

##### University vs Business Comparison:

| Criteria | University | Business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data | Clean, small, available | Multiple sources, dirty, missing, different definitions, constantly changing |
| Requirements | Clear, 5-6 requirements | Constantly changing, unclear |
| Time | Have several months | *"Can I have it tomorrow?"* |
| Team | 4-5 people, familiar | Work with multiple departments (Product, Marketing, Finance, DevOps, Security) |
| Consequence | Lose points, retake | Affects production, costs money, lose job |

##### 90% of student projects only do the "surface":

- Read data → transform → load → dashboard = **only 10%**
- **Missing:** handling pipeline failures, data quality, security, monitoring, alerting

##### How to improve:

> Use AI (Kiro, Claude) to scan your project and compare with the Well-Architected Framework, ask it to suggest missing production features.

##### Mr. Vinh's 5-year journey:

| Company | Lesson |
| --- | --- |
| Sieu Viet Group (startup) | **Ownership** – built from scratch, responsible for every decision |
| Heineken (large corporation) | **Business understanding** – the same word "active user" means different things to DevOps, Marketing, Finance |
| VNG ZaloPay (fintech) | **Think big, think future** – every line of code must accommodate future growth, cannot let the system crash |
| Renova Cloud (consulting) | **Start with problem** – choose tool/service after understanding the problem, don't choose the tool first |

##### Can AI replace data engineers?

- **AI can replace:** writing SQL, writing code, creating simple dashboards, writing documentation
- **AI cannot replace:** understanding business problems, designing architecture, decision making, communication, aligning processes between teams

##### 5 valuable lessons from Mr. Vinh:

1. **Connect the dots** – everything has meaning, even failures
2. **Be persistent** – don't give up after just a few dozen CV submissions
3. **Growth mindset** – be open to learning from everyone, everywhere
4. **Communication is a technical skill**, not a soft skill
5. **AI won't replace you**, but people who know how to use AI will replace those who don't

#### 3. Fear & Soft Skills (Ms. Nhu Tran - Account Manager, AWS Vietnam)

##### Common fears:

- **Fear of failure:** actually fear of consequences (low grades, judgmental looks, family disappointment)
- **Fear of public speaking:** fear of being judged, fear of saying something wrong

##### How to overcome:

- **Correctly identify your fear**
- **Practice** – no one is good at public speaking from the first time
- **Communicate with parents:** sometimes parents' expectations are simply *"live peacefully, happily, come home to have dinner with us"*

##### How to small talk with your boss:

- Start with a greeting – your boss will never greet you first
- *"Have you had breakfast yet?"*, *"Did you have a good weekend?"*, *"Is there anything I can help with this week?"*
- **Visibility starts with the smallest things**

##### The story of 11 attempts applying to AWS:

- Ms. Nhu applied to AWS **11 times**, rejected the first 10 times
- The 11th time, a "senior" at AWS referred her directly to the Hiring Manager
- The senior told the HM: *"I have someone quite interesting. Here are the traits she has that are hard to train. Here are the traits she lacks but can be trained (coachable)"*
- In the meeting, she said: *"Among all the candidates you're hiring, has anyone found a way to sit in front of you like I have? And I'm not in Singapore, I'm in Vietnam."*
- In the following interview, the HM **coached** her on how to interview instead of just asking questions

##### Lessons about opportunity:

- Publicly posted opportunities are **"Red Ocean"** – highly competitive
- The best opportunities are **"Blue Ocean"** – few people see them, require doing things no one wants to do

> *"Opportunity doesn't discriminate, it belongs to those who want it the most"*

> *"Seeds of opportunity planted today may sprout in the future"*

#### 4. Skill & Mindset for the AI Era (Mr. Khang Nguyen - Cloud Kinetics, Swinburne alumni)

##### Recruitment reality:

- **Last year:** chatbot assignment, 1 week deadline
- **This year:** same assignment + harder, 3 days deadline
- **Result:** 90% of submissions used AI but candidates didn't understand anything → rejected

> *"You can outsource your thinking, but you cannot outsource your understanding."*

##### How to use AI correctly:

- Use AI to **accelerate**, not to **replace thinking**
- Must understand **why** AI reached that conclusion
- Have foundational knowledge to **validate AI's output**

##### AI is just an amplifier:

- If you understand incorrectly → AI leads you in the wrong direction
- If you have a good foundation → AI helps you accomplish **many times** more work

##### Advice for students:

- **Ask WHY questions** – understand the fundamental layers, don't memorize
- **Embrace mistake** – making mistakes in school is normal, don't be afraid; at work, you're paid to not make mistakes
- **Stay hungry** – exceed expectations; 10 modules is not enough, find modules 11, 12, 13 yourself
- **Integrity** – do the parts that don't have points but truly need to be done; employers will ask about those parts
- **Don't go alone** – form teams with both technical and business people to gain multiple perspectives
- **Consistency** – be persistent in learning and development

##### Salary and job benefits:

> **Don't overemphasize starting salary**

**3 circles to evaluate a job:**

1. **Passion** – what do you enjoy doing?
2. **Responsibility** – things you have to do even if you don't enjoy them
3. **Benefits** – salary, network, knowledge, personal growth

> *"The first job is a springboard – gain experience, network, knowledge for the next salary level"*

##### 5 criteria employers evaluate (in order of priority):

| Order | Criteria | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Attitude** | Most important, shows learning potential |
| 2 | **Competence** | Professional skills |
| 3 | **Experience** | Number of years (unit of time) |
| 4 | **Exposure** | Breadth, how many projects, how many types of customers – **more important than experience** |
| 5 | **Talent** | Innate aptitude |

### Lessons Learned

After participating in the "Workshop: Cloud & AI Trends - Preparing for Students", I gained many valuable lessons about the job market, necessary skills, and development mindset in the AI era:

#### 1. The job market is changing rapidly

- **Increasing requirements**: Interns already need to know Kubernetes, businesses no longer have patience for Freshers. Students need to equip themselves with practical knowledge early on.
- **Cloud is an inevitable trend**: AWS has grown 20 times in 6 years, Cloud First is every business's strategy. Need to master cloud knowledge to avoid falling behind.
- **Networking is crucial**: 90% of jobs are not publicly posted. Joining communities, building networks is a survival factor for finding opportunities.

#### 2. The difference between university and business

- **Real data is not clean**: University provides cleaned data, but business data comes from multiple sources, missing, dirty, and constantly changing.
- **Requirements change constantly**: University has clear requirements, business requirements change constantly, are unclear, and always urgent.
- **Student projects only do 10%**: 90% of students only do the surface part (read data → transform → load → dashboard), missing important parts like: failure handling, data quality, security, monitoring, alerting.

#### 3. Mindset and skills needed in the AI era

- **AI is an amplifier**: If you understand incorrectly → AI leads you in the wrong direction. If you have a good foundation → AI helps you accomplish many times more work.
- **Don't outsource thinking**: You can use AI to accelerate, but you can't use AI to replace understanding. Must understand why AI reached that conclusion.
- **Communication is a technical skill**: Communication is no longer a soft skill but a technical skill needed to work effectively in business.

#### 4. Fear and how to overcome it

- **Fear of failure and public speaking is normal**: Need to correctly identify fears and practice regularly to overcome them.
- **Visibility starts with the smallest things**: Small talk with your boss, greeting colleagues, building relationships through small actions.
- **Opportunities are in the Blue Ocean**: Publicly posted opportunities are Red Ocean - highly competitive. The best opportunities come from doing things no one wants to do.

#### 5. Personal development direction

- **Ask WHY questions**: Understand fundamental layers, don't memorize.
- **Embrace mistake**: Making mistakes in school is normal, don't be afraid. At work, you're paid to not make mistakes.
- **Stay hungry**: Exceed expectations, seek additional knowledge beyond the curriculum.
- **Integrity**: Do the parts that don't have points but truly need to be done.
- **Consistency**: Be persistent in learning and development.
- **Don't overemphasize starting salary**: The first job is a springboard to gain experience, network, knowledge for the next salary level.

### Conclusion

The event provided a comprehensive and practical perspective on the Cloud & AI job market, necessary skills, and development mindset in the AI era. From the speakers' stories, I realized that:

1. **The market is changing fast** – need to continuously update and develop yourself
2. **Solid foundation is key** – AI is just a tool, foundational knowledge is what matters
3. **Soft skills determine success** – communication, visibility, consistency are factors that make you stand out
4. **Never stop learning and developing** – the learning journey doesn't stop when you graduate

This is the golden time to equip the knowledge, skills, and mindset needed to be ready for the future job market. The event gave me more motivation and clearer direction for my career path.
